# Quokka Dream Spirit Reference

- Approved silhouette: Q-B1
- View: front three-quarter
- Head share of total height: approximately 62%
- Body: extra-wide compact pear shape, shortened torso, grounded stance
- Ears: small rounded crescent dream sensors; not literal animal ears
- Tail: one compact cloud-like tail
- Face: two large near-black eyes, lower 20~30% lavender iris, one large and two small crisp white highlights, short eye-to-mouth distance, tiny open smile, round pink cheeks
- Dream motif: navy crescent mark on the forehead and restrained star particles
- Palette: warm cream base, lavender shadow, pale navy accent
- Maximum visible colors: 16
- Exclusions: ghost tail, floating lower body, clothing, props, realistic fur, gradients, extra limbs

## Approval Notes

- Keep the original Q-B silhouette's broad cheeks and huggable width.
- Both short feet must remain visibly planted so the character reads as alive rather than spectral.
- Express the dream-spirit identity through the crescent sensors, forehead mark, palette, and subtle particles.
- Do not remove or taper the lower body into smoke, flame, or a ghost form.
- Keep the arms close to the body and omit fingers, toes, and claws.
- The crescent dream sensors are the single identifying silhouette part; the forehead and chest marks must remain secondary.
- Keep the early-stage body palette restrained; add saturated pink, blue, yellow, and gold accents through the sensors, eyes, tail, dream mark, and constellation patterns as the character evolves.
- The fifth stage may be colorful, but each color must have one clear role and the body base color must remain recognizable.
