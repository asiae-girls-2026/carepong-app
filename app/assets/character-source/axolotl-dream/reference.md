# Axolotl Dream Spirit Reference

- Approved silhouette: A-A
- View: front three-quarter
- Body anchors: shared with quokka-dream
- Head share of total height: approximately 62%
- Head: wide, softly rounded shape
- Feelers: three short rounded prongs on each side forming compact starbursts
- Tail: one compact sky-blue wave tail
- Face: two large near-black eyes, lower 20~30% violet-blue iris, one large and two small white highlights, tiny open smile, cool-pink cheeks
- Dream motif: small four-point yellow star core on chest
- Palette: pale blush-pink base, sky-blue secondary color, deep violet outline, small star-yellow accent
- Maximum visible colors: 16
- Exclusions: realistic gills, wet texture, exposed anatomy, crescent ear sensors, cloud tail, clothing, props, gradients, extra limbs

## Approval Notes

- Keep the total height, eye baseline, shoulder and hip anchors, and ground line compatible with quokka-dream.
- The three-prong star-dream feelers are the single identifying silhouette part.
- Feelers must read as magical sensory organs rather than realistic external gills.
- Keep the wave tail smaller than the head and close to the body so it does not dominate movement frames.
- Maintain two planted feet; never taper the lower body into smoke, flame, or a ghost form.
